A small AI agency that does the work itself

Dominion AI Agency is an AI agency for small business owners who want the benefits of AI on the phone and on the website without becoming software administrators.

We set up and manage the tools: an AI receptionist that answers your calls around the clock, answers common questions from the facts you give us, takes messages and books appointments; a chat and voice widget for your website; pipelines and email follow-up in a CRM; review requests; and, when you need one, a website. Everything is built on HighLevel, a widely used platform for small business marketing and automation, so you are never stuck with a homemade system nobody else can maintain.

We are not a software company and we do not sell you a login and wish you luck. We configure the system, test it, adjust it as your business changes, and tell you plainly what it can and cannot do.

How working with an AI agency goes here

No setup time is promised up front, because it depends on how much of your business the system has to know. The order of the work does not change.

  1. You call or write. Call the main line and talk to the AI receptionist, or send the short form on the contact page. We reply by phone or email.
  2. We learn your business. Your services, prices you want quoted (or not), hours, the questions callers ask, who should get which messages, and the calendar appointments go into.
  3. We build and test it. The agent is configured on HighLevel with your facts, then tested with real practice calls until it answers the way you want.
  4. It goes on your line. Usually by forwarding your existing number, so customers keep calling the number they already know.
  5. We keep it current. New services, holiday hours and questions it handled badly get fixed as part of the monthly service.
