Who we are

Dominion AI Agency is a d/b/a of Dominion Digital Group, a Black-owned company in East Texas that Maurice Johnson founded in 2022.

We are a small AI automation agency, not a software company. We do not sell you a login. We set up AI receptionists and voice agents, website chat and voice widgets, appointment booking, CRM pipelines with email follow-up, review requests and websites for small businesses, and then we keep them running.

Maurice sets up each system. When you talk to us about your business, you are talking to the person who will configure your agent.

Why our own phone line is answered by AI

It would be strange to sell AI receptionists and then send our own calls to voicemail. Our main line is answered around the clock by an AI receptionist built the same way as the ones we set up for clients.

That gives you something most sales pages cannot: a working sample you can test on your own terms. Call at an odd hour. Ask it something off script. Notice what it does well and where it hands off. If you would not want something like it answering your customers, you have learned that for free.

Why we build on HighLevel

Every system we set up runs on HighLevel, a widely used platform for small business marketing and automation. It covers the pieces our clients need in one place: voice AI agents, conversation AI for chat, calendars, a CRM with pipelines, workflows and email.

Building on an established platform rather than a homemade tool means your setup is not a mystery only one person understands. The platform has its own documentation and support, and the pieces it uses are standard.

We also link to HighLevel on our tools page, with a clear note that those are referral links.

What we are, and what we are not

We are

  • A small agency that does the setup and the upkeep itself
  • Built on HighLevel
  • Remote, serving all 50 states from East Texas
  • Upfront about prices, which are on the pricing page
  • Plain about limits, which every service page lists

We are not

  • A call center, or a staff of human receptionists
  • A software company building custom AI models
  • A source of legal, medical or tax advice
  • A HIPAA compliance program
  • A company that will invent reviews, client counts or results to sell you

Texting, stated up front

Some of what we set up depends on business texting, which requires carrier registration. Ours is not approved yet, so we say so on every page that mentions it rather than in fine print.
