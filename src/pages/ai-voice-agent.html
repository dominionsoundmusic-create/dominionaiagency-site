Inbound calls: an AI voice agent for customer service

Inbound is where nearly every business starts. Your existing number forwards to the agent (or rings you first and rolls to it), and the agent handles the conversation from there. Good AI for customer service on the phone is mostly unglamorous work done consistently:

  • Answering the questions you hear every week: hours, service area, whether you handle a certain job, what to bring, where to park
  • Taking a complete message, with name, number and the reason for the call, and emailing it to the right person
  • Booking, moving or canceling an appointment on a calendar you control (see AI appointment booking)
  • Transferring the caller to a person when the call needs one, or to a second agent set up for a different job

An AI voice agent for customer support does not make a business friendlier on its own. It repeats whatever it is given. If your policies are vague, its answers will be vague, so a large part of our setup work is getting your answers written down clearly first.

What we configure inside the agent

An AI voice agent for business is only as good as five settings. These are the parts we build and adjust for you, and the parts you approve.

  • The voiceWe pick a voice that suits the business and, where the caller base needs it, set the agent to work in more than one language, such as English and Spanish. We test each language before it goes live rather than assuming it works.
  • The scriptHow the agent opens the call, what it says it is, the tone it keeps, and the questions it asks before it acts. We write it with you and rewrite it after listening to real calls.
  • The knowledgeYour services, hours, policies and the answers you want given word for word. HighLevel lets the agent answer from a connected knowledge base, so changing a fact in one place changes it on every call.
  • The actionsWhat the agent is allowed to do: book on a calendar, update a contact, start a workflow, send you a recap email. If an action is not switched on, the agent cannot take it.
  • The handoffWhen a person should take over (an angry caller, an emergency, a question outside the script), where the call goes, and what happens after hours when nobody can.

A custom AI voice agent in this sense is custom in its content and rules, not in its code. We do not build AI models or new software. We configure HighLevel's Voice AI with your business, which is also why the setup can be maintained by someone other than us if you ever need that.

Outbound AI voice agent calls, and the consent they need

On AI Pro the agent can also place calls. On HighLevel an outbound call is triggered by a workflow, for example a reminder the day before a booked visit, or a callback to someone who asked for a quote on your website. HighLevel requires the account to accept its outbound calling terms and meet its verification steps first, and it limits outbound Voice AI calls to between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM in the contact's time zone, based on their phone number.

The legal side matters more than the settings. In February 2024 the Federal Communications Commission issued a declaratory ruling (FCC 24-17) confirming that the Telephone Consumer Protection Act's limits on calls using an "artificial or prerecorded voice" cover current AI technologies that generate human voices. In plain terms, a call where an AI voice speaks is treated like a robocall, and it generally needs the called person's prior express consent unless an emergency purpose or an exemption applies. Sales calls carry extra duties: the FTC says sellers and telemarketers must remove numbers on the National Do Not Call Registry from their lists at least every 31 days, and some states keep their own lists.

What that means for how we set it up:

  • We only build outbound calls to contacts who came to you and agreed to be called, and we record where that consent came from
  • We do not load purchased lists or set up AI voice agent cold calling to strangers
  • The agent says who it is calling for, and a person who asks not to be called again is marked so the calls stop
  • HighLevel's own documentation says the consent duty rests with the business, so you decide, with your own attorney, whether a given outbound plan fits the rules that apply to you

This is a summary of public rules, not legal advice. AI voice agent for sales use cases, in particular, are where you want a lawyer's read before anything dials.

One agent, or several working together

A single agent can take inbound calls and, on AI Pro, make outbound ones. Some businesses are better served by splitting the work. On AI Empire we set up multiple AI voice agents, each with a narrower job and a script that fits it. A few ways owners divide it up:

  • A front agent that greets and sorts callers, and a booking agent that only handles scheduling
  • One agent for new customers asking about prices and availability, another for existing customers with a question about a job already booked
  • A separate agent for an after-hours line with a shorter script and a clear emergency instruction

HighLevel lets one agent hand a live call to another, and the summary, transcript and recap email cover everything said across both. Splitting is not automatically better: each extra agent is another script to keep current, so we suggest it only when one script would get crowded.

Is an AI voice agent the right call for you?

It tends to fit when

  • Many calls ask the same handful of questions
  • Calls come in while you are on a job, with a customer or closed
  • You book appointments and can say clearly which slots are open
  • You follow up with people who asked to hear from you, and the follow-up keeps slipping

How we put an agent on your line

  1. A conversation about your calls. Who calls, what they ask, what you want done with each kind of call, and what must always go to you.
  2. A first build. We write the script, load your knowledge, connect the calendar and the actions, and choose the voice.
  3. Test calls. We call it the way your customers would, including the strange ones, and fix what it gets wrong. You call it too.
  4. Go live by forwarding. Your customers keep dialing the number they know. Outbound calling, if you have it, starts only after the consent records are in place.
  5. Monthly upkeep. We read call summaries, adjust answers, and change the script when your hours, prices or services change.

We do not promise a go-live date up front, because it depends on how much the agent needs to know. The how it works page has the longer version.
