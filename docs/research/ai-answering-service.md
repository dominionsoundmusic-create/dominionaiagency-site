## Call forwarding (carrier)
- Verizon, Call Forwarding: Everything You Need to Know,
  https://www.verizon.com/articles/verizon-unlimited-plans/call-forwarding-everything-you-need-to-know/
  - "Dial *71 and the number you want to forward calls to" sets No Answer/Busy Transfer; calls forward when the
    line is busy or unanswered after three or four rings; "To deactivate the No Answer/Busy Transfer feature, just
    dial *73." Conditional forwarding can also be set in My Verizon. Airtime charges apply to forwarded calls
    (not used on the page). Calls cannot be forwarded to international numbers (not used).
  - Used: the forwarding paragraph and the "keep my number" FAQ. Page says codes differ by carrier and we test.
  - T-Mobile codes (**61*, **67*, **62*) were found only on third-party pages, so they were NOT used.
  - No FCC consumer page on call forwarding was found, so none is cited.
