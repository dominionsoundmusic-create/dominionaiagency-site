# Research notes: /ai-for/dentists.html (dental ai receptionist)
Direct fetches blocked by the session proxy; facts confirmed through search results quoting the sources.

1. HHS, Business Associates guidance: a business associate is a person or entity that performs certain functions or
   activities involving the use or disclosure of PHI on behalf of, or provides services to, a covered entity;
   workforce members are not business associates; a subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains or transmits PHI on
   behalf of a business associate is also a business associate.
   https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/business-associates/index.html
2. HHS, Sample Business Associate Agreement Provisions (Jan 25 2013): "The HIPAA Rules generally require that covered
   entities and business associates enter into contracts with their business associates to ensure that the business
   associates will appropriately safeguard protected health information." Sample only, not required wording.
   https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/covered-entities/sample-business-associate-agreement-provisions/index.html
3. HHS FAQ 198, May health care providers leave messages: permitted; HHS suggests limiting messages, e.g. leaving only
   name and number and other information necessary to confirm an appointment, or asking the individual to call back.
   https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/faq/198/may-health-care-providers-leave-messages/index.html
4. Cochrane review CD004346 on recall intervals: for adults, six-month check-ups vs personalised intervals based on the
   dentist's assessment of risk make no difference to tooth decay, gum disease or quality of life; intervals up to 24
   months may not harm these outcomes. https://www.cochrane.org/CD004346/ORAL_how-often-should-people-have-their-teeth-checked-dentist
   Used: recall intervals are set by the dentist per patient, so the AI does not pick them.
5. HHS minimum necessary requirement (45 CFR 164.502(b), 164.514(d)): reasonable steps to limit use/disclosure of
   PHI to the minimum necessary. https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/minimum-necessary-requirement/index.html
