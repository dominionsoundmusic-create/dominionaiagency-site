# Research notes: /ai-for/law-firms.html (ai for law firms)
Direct fetches blocked by the session proxy; facts confirmed through search results quoting the sources.

1. ABA Formal Opinion 512, "Generative Artificial Intelligence Tools", issued July 29, 2024 by the Standing Committee
   on Ethics and Professional Responsibility; the ABA's first formal ethics guidance on generative AI.
   https://www.americanbar.org/news/abanews/aba-news-archives/2024/07/aba-issues-guidance-lawyers-use-ai-tools/
   Covers competence (1.1), confidentiality (1.6), communication (1.4), meritorious claims and candor, supervision
   (5.1, 5.3) and fees (1.5). Quote reported in coverage: "merely adding general, boiler-plate provisions to engagement
   letters purporting to authorize the lawyer to use GAI is not sufficient." Supervisory lawyers should set policies and
   train staff; lawyers should evaluate the confidentiality and security of tools. Formal Opinions are not binding on
   courts or disciplinary bodies.
2. Model Rule 5.3, Responsibilities Regarding Nonlawyer Assistance: managers make reasonable efforts to ensure the firm
   has "measures giving reasonable assurance" that nonlawyer conduct is compatible with the lawyer's obligations.
   https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_5_3_responsibilities_regarding_nonlawyer_assistant/
3. Model Rule 1.18, Duties to Prospective Client: a person who consults with a lawyer about possibly forming a
   client-lawyer relationship is a prospective client; information learned may not be used or revealed (except as 1.9
   permits); (c) conflict bar where significantly harmful information was received; (d) screening exception requires
   that the lawyer took reasonable measures to avoid exposure to more disqualifying information than reasonably necessary.
   https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_1_18_duties_of_prospective_client/
4. Model Rule 7.3, Solicitation of Clients: (b) a lawyer shall not solicit professional employment by live
   person-to-person contact when a significant motive is pecuniary gain, with listed exceptions. Comment [2]: live
   telephone and other real-time person-to-person communications.
   https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_7_3_direct_contact_with_prospective_clients/
5. Model Rule 1.6(c): reasonable efforts to prevent the inadvertent or unauthorized disclosure of, or unauthorized
   access to, information relating to the representation of a client.
   https://www.americanbar.org/groups/professional_responsibility/publications/model_rules_of_professional_conduct/rule_1_6_confidentiality_of_information/
6. FCC Feb 8 2024 Declaratory Ruling: AI voices are "artificial" under the TCPA; prior express consent required.
   https://www.fcc.gov/document/fcc-makes-ai-generated-voices-robocalls-illegal
