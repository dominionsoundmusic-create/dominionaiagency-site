## FCC declaratory ruling, AI voices under the TCPA
- FCC 24-17, CG Docket No. 23-362, adopted February 2, 2024, released/announced February 8, 2024.
- Quote: "In this Declaratory Ruling, we confirm that the TCPA's restrictions on the use of 'artificial or
  prerecorded voice' encompass current AI technologies that generate human voices."
- Such calls need the called party's prior express consent unless an emergency purpose or exemption applies.
- News release title: "FCC Makes AI-Generated Voices in Robocalls Illegal" (effective immediately).
- URLs: https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-24-17A1.pdf ;
  https://www.fcc.gov/document/fcc-makes-ai-generated-voices-robocalls-illegal
- Used on page: the ruling, its date (February 2024), "artificial" voice, prior express consent, check with counsel.
  NOT used: the August 2024 proposed disclosure rules (not confirmed final).

## FTC: National Do Not Call Registry for sales calls
- Businesses that sell by phone generally must access the Registry and remove listed numbers; "Sellers and
  telemarketers must delete all numbers in the Registry from their lists at least every 31 days."
- Some states run their own do not call lists; the TSR does not preempt state law.
- URL: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/qa-telemarketers-sellers-about-dnc-provisions-tsr-0
- Used: the 31 day refresh and the existence of state lists, stated generally.

## HighLevel Voice AI (platform docs)
- Create, Test, and Deploy a Voice AI Agent:
  https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000004107-how-to-create-voice-ai-agents
  A Voice AI Agent works from the instructions, business information and actions configured; answers from a
  connected Knowledge Base; can transfer callers to a person or to another Voice AI Agent; prep list includes
  approved business information, calendar, workflow, transfer number.
- Voice AI Outbound Calling:
  https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006598-voice-ai-outbound-calling
  Outbound calls are placed from workflow actions with a chosen agent and number; outbound calling terms must be
  accepted and location verification met; "supported calling window is 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM based on the
  contact's phone-number timezone"; newer docs say the platform does not perform contact consent validation
  for the business, so the business carries the consent duty.
- Voice AI Agent Transfer: https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000007796-voice-ai-agent-transfer
  Call summaries, transcripts, recap emails and workflow triggers include activity from all agents on the call.
- Multi-language: https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000009013-multi-language-support-for-voice-ai-agents
  Language picker allows one language or a set combination; some languages cannot be combined. Page says only
  that an agent can be set to more than one language, such as English and Spanish, and that we test it.
- Call logs: summary, transcript, recording (where one exists):
  https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000005900-call-logs-for-voice-ai-agents
