# Research notes: /ai-appointment-booking/ (Oct 15 2026)

(WebFetch blocked in this container; facts confirmed from WebSearch results restricted to help.gohighlevel.com.)

- Appointment Booking in Voice AI:
  https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000005293-appointment-booking-for-voice-ai-agents-in-highlevel
  "Voice AI can schedule appointments directly during calls using the Appointment Booking action." Can book on a
  single calendar or route across several eligible calendars. If enabled, it can handle changes by verifying
  caller details and updating the original calendar.
- Book across multiple calendars (intent-based):
  https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000007203-highlevel-voice-ai-book-across-multiple-calendars-intent%E2%80%91based-selection-
  The AI matches the caller's intent to a calendar; a fallback calendar catches requests that match none;
  linked or conflict calendars prevent double booking.
- Conversation AI appointment booking (chat): https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000000210-appointment-booking-in-conversation-ai
- Linked and conflict calendars:
  https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000002374-setting-up-linked-calendars-conflict-calendars
  External events block availability only when marked Busy; events marked Free leave the slot open; write-back
  to several calendars can make duplicates, so one primary work calendar is recommended; two-way mode turns
  external events into appointments.
- Google Calendar integration: https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000002369
- Outlook sync: https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/48001188796-how-to-sync-highlevel-calendars-with-outlook
  Primary calendar reads and writes; "Check for Conflicts" only reads; Google and Outlook can be mixed.
- iCloud: one-way only: https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000001477-how-to-connect-icloud-with-highlevel-
- Calendar notifications: https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000003441-calendar-email-in-app-sms-whatsapp-appointment-notifications
  Booking confirmations, cancellations, reschedules, reminders and follow-ups by email, SMS or in-app. On our
  page: email now, texting after carrier approval.
