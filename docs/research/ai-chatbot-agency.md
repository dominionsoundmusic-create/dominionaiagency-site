## HighLevel Conversation AI and the chat widget
- Conversation AI Bot explained: https://help.gohighlevel.com/en/support/solutions/articles/155000001335
  Once the bot is built and "Web Chat" is added as a supported channel, the AI is included automatically when the
  chat widget is installed on the website.
- Live chat widget: https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000005067-getting-started-set-up-a-live-chat-widget
  Created under Sites > Chat Widget; copy the embed code; lets visitors start conversations with your team or
  Conversation AI.
- Human handover action: https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000005615-conversation-ai-human-handover-action
  Moves a conversation from the bot to a person when the bot reaches a limit, such as a complex question,
  repeated misunderstanding, or a request for a person; can assign a team member, create a follow-up task,
  send a closing message and pause the bot.
- Knowledge sources: https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006456-conversation-ai-new-knowledge-sources-quality-upgrades
  Source types include tables, rich text, file upload, FAQ, web crawler and web search; admins can view the
  knowledge chunks behind a reply.
- Voice AI widget: https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/155000006056-how-to-use-the-voice-ai-voice-widget
  Separate widget type (Sites > Chat Widget > New, Voice AI); script pasted into the site header or footer;
  "Call transfer is not yet supported while in beta."

## Rule-based vs AI chatbots
- IBM, Types of Chatbots: https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/chatbot-types
  Rule-based bots run on if-then logic and scripted replies, users limited to simple phrases or buttons; AI
  chatbots use NLP and large language models to understand intent however a question is phrased.
- Used as general background only; IBM not named in copy.
