# Research notes: /ai-for/ (AI for small business, industries hub)
Researched Oct 15 2026. Direct page fetches were blocked by this session's network proxy (americanbar.org, hhs.gov,
census.gov refused); every fact below was confirmed through web search results that quote the source page. The
independent fact-check should open each URL.

1. U.S. Census Bureau, "Large Firms With at Least 20 Employees Biggest AI Users" (May 2026), BTOS data Dec 14 2025 to
   May 3 2026. https://www.census.gov/library/stories/2026/05/ai-use-businesses.html
   - "overall AI usage hovered between 17% and 20%"
   - "37% of firms with at least 250 employees reported using AI"; "Fewer than 20% of firms with four or fewer
     employees reported AI use" (search result paraphrase: "fewer than 20% of firms with four or fewer employees
     reported using AI").
   - Question wording changed Nov 2025 from AI use "in producing goods or services" to "in any business function".
   Used on page: the 17% to 20% range and the fewer-than-20% for four-or-fewer-employee firms, attributed to the BTOS.
2. SBA, Dec 11 2024 news release: Administrator Guzman launched "SBA's AI for Small Businesses Resource Hub" at the
   inaugural AI Small Business Summit at Georgia Tech. https://www.sba.gov/article/2024/12/11/sba-administrator-guzman-hosts-inaugural-artificial-intelligence-small-business-summit-georgia-tech
   Used: a free federal place to learn about AI.
3. FTC Business Blog, "Keep your AI claims in check" (Feb 2023). https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2023/02/keep-your-ai-claims-check
   Coverage: AI products must "work as advertised"; asks whether a company is exaggerating what its AI can do.
   Used: be skeptical of sales claims; ask for a demo.
4. FCC, Feb 8 2024 Declaratory Ruling: AI-generated voices are "artificial" under the TCPA; prior express consent
   needed for such calls. https://www.fcc.gov/document/fcc-makes-ai-generated-voices-robocalls-illegal
   Used: outbound AI calls need consent; we answer inbound first.
5. HighLevel official site (platform). https://www.gohighlevel.com/ (described generally only).
Not used: secondary-site AI adoption figures (21.7%, 23.8%): could not confirm against Census itself.
